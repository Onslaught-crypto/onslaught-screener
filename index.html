How to read this · settings
  • DMI: ▲ when +DI > −DI and ADX ≥ threshold (trend has strength), ▼ when −DI > +DI and ADX ≥ threshold, · when ADX is below threshold.
  • RSI > EMA: RSI(14) above its own EMA means momentum is improving. RSI > 50: momentum regime.
  • OBV > EMA50: volume flow is confirming price.
  • OI rising: open interest grew by at least the threshold over N candles. New positions are fuelling the move, so it counts toward both the bull and the bear score.
  • MTF: trend direction on 1D, 4H and 1H (3+ of the 4 TA checks). All three agreeing with an aligned setup adds to its rank.
  • Triggers are events in the last N candles (subscript = candles ago): DMI cross (ADX ≥ threshold − 5), RSI crossing 50, RSI crossing its EMA, OBV crossing its EMA, squeeze fired (Bollinger 20/2 leaving Keltner 20/1.5 ATR), Coil (squeeze still on), RVOL (volume ≥ 2× the 20-bar average), and regular or hidden RSI divergences from 3-bar pivots. Green = bullish, red = bearish.
  • ★ Watchlist: star a pair to pin it to the top (Pin ★) or filter to it (★ only, or the Watchlist card). Stars are saved in your browser.
  • Since last scan: pairs that became aligned or triggered (⚡) since your previous scan on the same timeframe, and pairs that lost alignment. New entries get an IN pill and a purple edge in the table. Click a chip to open its trade plan.
  • Alerts: turn on with 🔔 (your browser asks permission). After each scan, new triggered setups (or new alignments too, in Settings) raise a desktop notification, an on-page toast and a sound; the tab title shows a count while you're away. Auto-refresh must be on and the tab must stay open; with alerts on, scans keep running in a background tab.
  • LONG / SHORT badge = a triggered setup: 4–5/5 aligned plus a trigger in the same direction. These rank first and have their own stat card.
  • Positioning compares price and OI change over the same N candles: New longs (price ↑ OI ↑), New shorts (price ↓ OI ↑), Short covering (price ↑ OI ↓, a squeeze that often fades once covering ends), Long liquidation (price ↓ OI ↓). Moves under 0.5% count as flat.
  • Funding: the last funding rate. 🔥 marks extremes: at or above the hot threshold longs are crowded, at or below the cold threshold shorts are crowded. Crowded sides are fuel for squeezes against them.
  • Regime bar: BTC trend on 4H and 1D (3+ of the 4 TA checks), breadth across the scanned pairs, and median funding and OI. Aligned setups against the regime get a dashed outline on their score.
  • Ext (ATR): how far the close is from EMA20, measured in ATRs. Above +3 or below −3 (⚠) means the move is stretched. The trend may be real, but entries there have poor risk/reward, so wait for a pullback.
  • Aligned = 4 or 5 of 5 checks in one direction. NEW = alignment appeared on the latest candle (it wasn't aligned on the one before).
  • Hover any check to see the raw values. Click a row to open its trade plan (chart, entry, stop, targets, sizing). The ↗ next to a pair opens TradingView.

Indicator lengths and ADX threshold apply on the next scan. Filters, OI threshold and N apply instantly.

Press Load sector flow to pull 24h volume and 30 days of daily candles, then compare each sector with its own normal.
How to read this
  • vs Week / vs Month: today's 24h volume divided by the sector's average day over the last 7 / 30 closed days. 1.0 is normal, 2.0 is double. Both high = a real move with fuel. Month high but week flat = it has been hot all week, you may be late. Week high but month flat = something just started.
  • Share Δ: the sector's slice of all volume shown today minus its average slice over the last 7 days, in percentage points. This is the rotation signal.
  • Participation: share of the sector's coins whose own volume is at least 1.5× their weekly normal. High volume with low participation means one coin is pumping, not the sector. This column kills most false alarms.
  • Price (VW): the volume-weighted 24h price change. Volume alone never says who is buying; paired with price, heavy volume and price up = money in, heavy volume and price down = money out.
  • Trend: how many of the sector's coins are bull- or bear-aligned in your last Trend Confirmation scan (scan first to fill it).
  • Coins carry several tags, so a coin's volume counts toward each of its sectors; shares are normalised across the sectors shown. Volume shows where attention is, not where price goes next. Confirm on the chart.
